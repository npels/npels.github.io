The Game:

https://npels.itch.io/cavern-blitz

"Delve into a dangerous cavern, fight monsters, and mine ores to gather materials. Use your resources to craft better gear and make it deeper and deeper into the cavern!"

Cavern Blitz was developed as a part of my Game Design and Development class at UC Berkeley in a team of 5 students, including me. In it, the player traverses through procedurally generated caverns while collecting resources that they can use to craft newer and better gear. Inspirations include Stardew Valley, roguelite games, and Terraria.

My Role:

Cavern Blitz was initially my idea, and I pitched it to the class for our final project. I acted as our team leader, running meetings, delegating tasks and responsibilities, deciding goals, and designing gameplay mechanics.

I was also a programmer on the project. I designed and implemented a system for procedurally generating cave levels, as well as the game's crafting system. I helped out in many other aspects of the game, such as camera work, mining, animation, movement, and combat.
