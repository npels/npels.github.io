Kindled was a personal project that I worked on beginning in the summer of 2020 in order to get more experience in Unity and game development in general. It's a puzzle/exploration game taking place in a mysterious underground temple. The game is currently unfinished, with only the base mechanics and a few levels completed. Maybe some day I will return to Kindled, but I have no plans to currently.

I developed Kindled completely solo, doing almost all of the scripting, art, modelling, and animation by myself. I created a system to modularly construct levels out of various premade 3D tiles that would allow me to build them quickly and easily.
