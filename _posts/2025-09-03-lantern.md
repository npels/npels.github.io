_Lantern: Caverns of Chaos_ is a roguelite mining/resource collection game with hack-and-slash combat, procedurally generated cave levels, and a crafting system to create unique weapons and accessories. The game has a heavy focus on cultivating mood and atmosphere with a distinct art style and realtime 2D lighting.

### Gameplay

![A screenshot of Lantern](/assets/img/lantern/lantern-screenshot-1.png)

The premise of the game is to delve into procedurally generated caves that contain resources to collect like ores and plants, as well as dangerous enemies.

![Look behind you!](/assets/img/lantern/lantern-screenshot-6.png)

You can then return to your home base to use the resources you collected in order to craft new weapons, accessories, and consumables which will make your next run easier and allow you to dig deeper and deeper into the caves.

![The crafting UI](/assets/img/lantern/lantern-screenshot-9.png)

As you progress, you'll be faced with unique trials, discover new resources, fight new enemies, and eventually uncover entirely new cave biomes.

![A few unique enemies](/assets/img/lantern/lantern-screenshot-8.png)

While currently in an unfinished state, it still contains a wide variety of items, enemies, and challenges and is, in my personal opinion, very fun to play. You can download a playable build on my itch page here: [https://npels.itch.io/lantern](https://npels.itch.io/lantern)

![A screenshot of Lantern](/assets/img/lantern/lantern-screenshot-3.png)

I designed and developed Lantern almost completely solo. Much of the art was either done by contracted artists or from purchased pixel art packs, though a good amount of the art for the items was made by me. I did all of the programming apart from the audio system.

![A screenshot of Lantern](/assets/img/lantern/lantern-screenshot-4.png)

I created the premise for this game (an extention of Cavern Blitz, an earlier project I led and worked on), and designed every aspect of the gameplay. This project was my baby for around 10 months, until funding for Saplink unfortunately ran dry and my contract expired. Nevertheless, I am very proud of what I was able to create in that time.
