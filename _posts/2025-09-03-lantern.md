Lantern: Caverns of Chaos is a roguelite mining/resource collection game with hack-and-slash combat, procedurally generated cave levels, and a crafting system to create unique weapons and accessories. While currently in an unfinished state, it still contains a wide variety of items, enemies, and challenges and is, in my personal opinion, very fun to play.

You can download a playable build on my itch page here: https://npels.itch.io/lantern

#### My Role

I designed and developed Lantern almost completely solo. Much of the art was either done by contracted artists or from purchased pixel art packs, though a good amount of the art for the items was made by me. I did all of the programming apart from the audio system.

I created the premise for this game (an extention of Cavern Blitz, an earlier project I led and worked on), and designed every aspect of the gameplay. This project was my baby for around 10 months, until funding for Saplink unfortunately ran dry and my contract expired. Nevertheless, I am very proud of what I was able to create in that time.
